My personal portfolio and app platform. Started as a résumé site, grew into a full-stack monorepo with auth, a custom design system, and way too many SCSS variables. No regrets.

Vue 3 on the front, .NET 10 on the back, PostgreSQL underneath — and a terminal aesthetic because I thought it was cool and I stand by that.

---

### What's inside

- 📄 **Currículo** — who I am, where I've worked, what I've learned
- 🔐 **Auth** — login, register, profile, refresh tokens and all that jazz — yes, it's almost done, no, I won't give you an ETA
- 🚧 **More modules** — in the backlog, where they live rent-free
